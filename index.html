Hello, my name is Pengfei Wu. I am an assistant professor with National Engineering Research Center for Software Engineering, Peking University. I obtained my bachelor’s and doctoral degree in Software Engineering from Shandong University and Peking University in 2016 and 2020, respectively. Before joining PKU, I was a senior research scientist with School of Computing and Information Systems, Singapore Management University, cooperated with Prof. Robert H. Deng (IEEE Fellow, AAIA Fellow, and SAEng Fellow), and a research fellow with School of Computing, National University of Singapore, cooperated with Prof. Ee-Chien Chang.

My research interests include applied cryptography, computer & information security, and privacy-enhancing techniques, etc. As (co-)first author, I have published at security venues including USENIX Security, NDSS, ESORICS, AsiaCCS, IEEE TDSC, and IEEE TSC. I received the Honorable Mention Award of USENIX Security in 2025 and "Best Hardware and Physics Paper" in Cybersecurity Award 2026.
