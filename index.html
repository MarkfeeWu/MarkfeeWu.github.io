Hello, my name is Pengfei Wu. I am an assistant professor with National Engineering Research Center for Software Engineering, Peking University. I obtained my bachelor’s and doctoral degree in Software Engineering from Shandong University and Peking University in 2016 and 2020, respectively. Before joining PKU, I was a senior research scientist with School of Computing and Information Systems, Singapore Management University, from September 2023 to August 2026, and a research fellow with School of Computing, National University of Singapore, from November 2020 to September 2023. My research interests include applied cryptography, computer & information security, and privacy-preserving, etc.
